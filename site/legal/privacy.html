Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete your data, unless we must keep it for accounting;
  • restrict or object to a particular use;
  • transfer your data to another provider;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave.

Write to privacy@davidrdobrovsky.store and we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Payment keys are held as encrypted secrets and are never present in the website code. Only people who need order data in order to ship your parcel can see it.
