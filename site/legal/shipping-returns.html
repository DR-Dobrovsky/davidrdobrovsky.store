Where we ship

We deliver to all European Union member states. Orders are dispatched from a warehouse inside the EU, so there are no customs formalities, import duties or handling fees for EU customers.

Your right to withdraw

As a consumer in the EU you may withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. To do so, tell us in writing before the 14 days are up — an email to hello@seoulsilk.store is enough. You may also use the model withdrawal form, but you do not have to.

After telling us, send the goods back within 14 days. You bear the direct cost of returning them.

Condition of returned goods

Please return items in a resalable condition with their packaging. You may unpack and inspect an item as you would in a shop. If you go beyond that and the value drops, we may deduct the loss from your refund.

For hygiene reasons, cosmetic products that have been opened or used cannot be returned once their seal is broken. The device itself can be returned even if you have tried it.

Refunds

We refund within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of proof that you sent them, whichever comes first. The refund goes to the payment method you used, including standard delivery you originally paid. We do not refund the extra cost of an express option you chose.

Faulty or damaged items

If something arrives damaged or develops a fault, contact us with a photo and your order number. You have a statutory two-year guarantee of conformity under EU law; this sits alongside, and does not replace, the 14-day withdrawal right.

Return address

Do not send anything back before contacting us — unannounced parcels can be refused. We will confirm the correct return address in writing.
