The real problem

You didn’t buy a bad mask. You had no way to get it in.

Bleach, colour and heat lift the outer layer of the hair and wash out the proteins underneath. Most of what you apply afterwards never gets past that outer layer.

Coloured & bleached

Lightening swells the strand and leaves gaps where protein used to be. Hair feels rough, dry and stretches before it snaps.

Heat every morning

Straighteners and dryers drive out moisture faster than a rinse-out conditioner can put it back. The ends go first.

Products that sit on top

Rich creams coat the surface and rinse away. Without warmth, the cuticle stays shut and the actives never reach the cortex.

The ritual

Three steps, ten minutes, once a week.

This is how Korean salons finish a repair treatment — warmth first, then product, then a cool seal. The device just brings that sequence home.

  1. Steam

    Glide the cordless steamer over damp, sectioned hair for about four minutes. Warm micro-steam softens and lifts the cuticle.

  2. Treat

    Work the Growus Damage Therapy treatment through mid-lengths and ends while the hair is still warm and receptive.

  3. Seal

    Rinse cool, then mist the no-wash treatment over the ends. The cuticle closes and holds the moisture in.

Before you order

How often should I use it?

Once a week is enough for most people. Twice if your hair is bleached or you use heat daily. More often is not better — protein treatments can make hair feel stiff if overused.

Does it work with my own conditioner?

Yes. The steam step is independent of the product. The Growus set is what we pair it with because it is a protein-and-moisture combination made for damaged hair, but any treatment benefits.

Where does my order ship from?

From our supplier’s warehouse inside the EU. Delivery is typically 2–5 working days and there are no customs charges or import paperwork for EU addresses.

Is it safe for coloured hair?

Yes. Steam is warm, not hot, and there is no direct heat plate. It is gentler on colour than a straightener or a hot dryer.

Will this stop my hair falling out?

No, and we will not claim otherwise. This is cosmetic hair care: it conditions, adds shine and helps treatments absorb. If you are worried about hair loss, please speak to a doctor or dermatologist.
